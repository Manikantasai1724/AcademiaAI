Optimization in Deep Learning: Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD).

The computational process of Stochastic Gradient Descent consists of three fundamental steps:
First, perform a forward pass through the network to compute the loss between predicted outputs and ground truth.
Second, execute a backward pass using backpropagation to compute the gradient of the loss with respect to all parameters.
Third, update the weights by subtracting the product of the learning rate and the computed gradient.
Mini-batch SGD balances variance and computational efficiency by computing gradients over subsets of training examples.
